# Solo Notes

## Definition

A private note app for individuals who want to turn scattered thoughts into a
small daily writing habit.

## Users and situations

Individuals use it alone at the end of the day.

## Problem and current alternatives

Thoughts stay scattered across paper and generic note apps.

## Promised change

Users finish each day with one coherent personal note.

## Core loop

Capture fragments, review them, and publish one private daily note.

## Capabilities and boundaries

- Notes are private to one person.

## Experience principles

- Writing should feel quiet and interruption-free.

## Success signals

- Users complete more daily notes without increasing editing time.

## Assumptions and unknowns

- Unknown: whether weekly reflection is useful.
