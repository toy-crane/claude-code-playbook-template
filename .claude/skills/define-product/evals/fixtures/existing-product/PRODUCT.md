# Scope Clear

## Definition

An app for independent freelance designers to agree on project scope with a
client before work begins.

## Users and situations

Freelance designers use it when a new client project begins or its requirements
change.

## Problem and current alternatives

Requirements are scattered across email and documents, so designers manually
assemble them and clients approve ambiguous prose.

## Promised change

Designer and client begin work from the same explicit included and excluded
scope.

## Core loop

Collect requirements, draft scope, clarify included and excluded items, then
receive client approval.

## Capabilities and boundaries

- A client can review and approve scope without creating an account.
- Project execution and design production remain outside the product.

## Experience principles

- Make inclusion, exclusion, and the meaning of approval unmistakable.

## Success signals

- Fewer than 10% of approved projects require rework caused by scope ambiguity
  within 30 days.

## Assumptions and unknowns

- Unknown: the pricing model.
