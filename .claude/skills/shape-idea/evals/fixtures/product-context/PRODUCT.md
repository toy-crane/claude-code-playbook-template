# Scope Clear

## Definition

An app for freelance designers to establish a clear client-approved project
scope before work begins.

## Users and situations

Designers prepare scope and clients review it when a project begins or its
requirements change.

## Problem and current alternatives

Requirements are scattered across messages and documents, and clients often
approve prose that leaves inclusion and exclusion ambiguous.

## Promised change

Both parties start work from the same explicit scope.

## Core loop

Collect requirements, draft scope, clarify included and excluded items, then
receive client approval.

## Capabilities and boundaries

- Clients review, ask for clarification, and approve without creating an
  account.
- Project execution and design production remain outside the product.

## Experience principles

- Keep included and excluded work unmistakable at every client touchpoint.
- Explain the next action without professional product-management terminology.

## Success signals

- Approval produces fewer later disputes about whether an item was in scope.

## Assumptions and unknowns

- Unknown: whether client comments need retention after final approval.
