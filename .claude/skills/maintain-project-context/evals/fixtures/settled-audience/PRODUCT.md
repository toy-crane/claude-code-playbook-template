# Scope Clear

## Definition

An app for independent freelance designers to agree on project scope with a
client.

## Users and situations

Freelance designers use it before starting client work.

## Problem and current alternatives

Requirements are scattered across email and documents and are manually turned
into ambiguous scope prose.

## Promised change

Designer and client begin work from the same explicit scope.

## Core loop

Collect requirements, draft scope, clarify included and excluded items, then
receive client approval.

## Capabilities and boundaries

- Clients can review and approve without creating an account.
- Project execution remains outside the product.

## Experience principles

- Make inclusion, exclusion, and the meaning of approval unmistakable.

## Success signals

- Approved projects require less rework caused by scope ambiguity.

## Assumptions and unknowns

- Unknown: how other studio members participate.
